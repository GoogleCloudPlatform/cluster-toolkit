Encryption keys

The customer-managed keys OFE has created, one per resource. OFE creates and edits nothing here; the only action it offers is revoking a key, which schedules its versions for destruction and deliberately stops the resource working. A key whose resource was torn down under ABANDON stays in Cloud KMS until someone removes it there, and keys cost nothing to keep.
